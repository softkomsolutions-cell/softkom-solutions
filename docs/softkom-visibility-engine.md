# Softkom Visibility Engine

## Purpose

Build a compounding acquisition channel for Softkom by finding commercial search demand, publishing useful problem-led pages, and routing every visitor into a measurable Softkom diagnostic or sales action.

The operating principle is:

**Search demand -> problem page -> diagnostic -> qualified lead -> paid review -> implementation -> recurring support**

This is intentionally different from a high-volume AI content factory. Softkom should publish only when there is a credible search/customer problem to own.

## Positioning

Core promise:

> Make the business easier to run.

Supporting promise:

> Softkom helps growing businesses reduce wasted effort, recover revenue and streamline operations through better systems, automation, integration and practical AI.

## Daily / Weekly Operating Loop

1. Research live Google results for South African commercial-intent searches.
2. Record competitors, SERP intent, local relevance and whether Softkom has a credible offer.
3. Prioritise opportunities where the searcher is close to buying or diagnosing a business problem.
4. Produce one strong page or article that answers the problem better than the current results.
5. Add a clear CTA into the relevant Softkom funnel.
6. Publish only after factual, legal, UX and mobile review.
7. Track impressions, clicks, CTR, enquiries and downstream revenue.
8. Improve winners; do not keep publishing around topics that show no commercial signal.

## Content Types

### Tier 1: Commercial problem pages
Direct buying or diagnostic intent. Highest priority.

Examples:
- automate quote follow-up South Africa
- invoice chasing automation South Africa
- reduce software subscription costs
- recover old sales leads
- business process automation Johannesburg
- reduce manual admin in business

Primary CTA must be a relevant Softkom review or diagnostic.

### Tier 2: Decision-support pages
Searchers are comparing approaches or deciding whether to act.

Examples:
- when should a business automate a process?
- automation vs hiring more admin staff
- CRM vs custom workflow system
- how much does business automation cost in South Africa?
- when should a business replace spreadsheets?

These should lead into Business Efficiency Review or Business Process Review.

### Tier 3: Authority / education
Useful for AI citations, long-tail search and trust, but only after Tier 1/2 coverage.

Examples:
- practical AI policy for South African SMEs
- POPIA considerations for business automation
- how to map a business process before automating it
- how to measure automation ROI

## Qualification Score

Score each idea 0-5 on:

- Commercial intent
- Fit to a current Softkom offer
- South African relevance
- SERP weakness / content gap
- Ability to demonstrate real expertise
- Funnel clarity

Publish Tier 1 first when total score is 22/30 or higher.

## Content Quality Rules

- Do not publish generic AI-written filler.
- Do not fabricate statistics, client outcomes or case studies.
- Cite current authoritative sources where claims need evidence.
- Use South African terminology and examples only where genuinely relevant.
- Prefer original frameworks, calculators, checklists, diagrams and decision tools.
- Explain where automation is *not* appropriate.
- Every page must have one primary action.
- Avoid keyword cannibalisation: one primary intent per page.
- No mass-generated city pages.
- No backlink schemes or link exchanges.
- Do not copy competitor wording or proprietary content.

## Page Template

1. Search-intent H1
2. 2-3 sentence answer to the problem
3. Symptoms / warning signs
4. Cost or consequence of leaving it unresolved
5. How the process works today
6. What can be simplified / automated
7. What should remain human-controlled
8. Practical next step
9. Relevant Softkom offer / diagnostic
10. FAQ based on real related questions
11. Internal links to adjacent problem pages

## Measurement

Primary:
- Search impressions
- Non-brand clicks
- CTR
- Qualified form submissions
- Calls / meetings
- Paid reviews
- Project revenue

Secondary:
- Average position
- AI assistant citations / mentions where observable
- Referring domains
- Engagement with diagnostics
- Funnel conversion by landing page

Do not optimise for article count.
