# Softkom Revenue & Efficiency Delivery Playbooks

Status: internal operating standard  
Owner: Softkom Solutions  
Purpose: make every commercial diagnostic and managed solution repeatable, measurable and easy to fulfil.

## Core operating model

Every offer follows the same sequence:

1. **Check** — understand the current business before recommending technology.
2. **Quantify** — measure money, time, risk or missed opportunity.
3. **Prioritise** — rank fixes by business value, effort and confidence.
4. **Fix** — implement only the highest-value changes.
5. **Manage** — monitor recurring risks, costs and workflows where ongoing attention creates value.

Softkom must not recommend a rebuild, new subscription, automation or AI assistant unless there is a clear business case.

# 1. Business Efficiency Review

## Customer promise

**Show us how your business works. We will show you where it can work better.**

The client does not need to diagnose the solution. Softkom reviews the systems, software and day-to-day processes already in use and identifies where money, time, opportunities or control are being lost.

## Intake

Collect before discovery:

- business name, industry, staff count and operating locations
- owner / sponsor and operational contact
- current software list
- monthly or annual software invoices where available
- core process list
- CRM / quoting / accounting / support / ecommerce tools
- recurring reports
- examples of spreadsheets used operationally
- common customer enquiries
- quote and lead follow-up process
- collections / overdue invoice process
- known bottlenecks
- known AI tools
- key people who own critical processes

Never require passwords in the intake form.

## Discovery session

Target: 60–90 minutes.

Ask the client to walk through the business in this order:

1. How a lead/customer enters.
2. How the business quotes or sells.
3. How work/orders are fulfilled.
4. How suppliers are engaged.
5. How invoicing and collection happen.
6. How management reporting is produced.
7. Which recurring tasks consume the most staff time.
8. Which spreadsheets, inboxes or WhatsApp groups are operationally important.
9. Which software products are paid for.
10. Which processes depend on one person.
11. Where AI tools are used.
12. What management wishes worked better.

Record facts, not assumptions.

## Opportunity scoring

Score each candidate improvement from 0–5 on:

- **Financial impact** — direct saving or recoverable revenue.
- **Time impact** — staff hours reduced.
- **Frequency** — how often the problem occurs.
- **Control/risk impact** — errors, access, compliance or continuity exposure.
- **Implementation ease** — how quickly a useful improvement can be deployed.
- **Evidence confidence** — strength of the data supporting the finding.

Priority score:

Financial x 25 + Time x 20 + Frequency x 15 + Control x 15 + Ease x 15 + Confidence x 10, divided by 5 to express as a percentage.

Priority bands:

- **80–100:** immediate action
- **60–79:** high-value next step
- **40–59:** worthwhile but secondary
- **0–39:** monitor / do not prioritise

## Efficiency dimensions

Rate the business 0–100 in six dimensions:

- Software spend efficiency — 15%
- Manual work and process efficiency — 20%
- Lead / revenue capture — 20%
- Systems integration — 15%
- AI usage and control — 15%
- Business continuity / key-person risk — 15%

The score is diagnostic, not a benchmark against other companies unless benchmark data is available.

## Required output

Deliver a **Business Efficiency Map** containing:

- executive summary
- current systems map
- top five leakage points
- estimated software waste
- estimated recurring manual hours
- sales / quote / collections leakage
- integration gaps
- AI usage/control findings
- key-person dependency findings
- ranked opportunity table
- quick wins
- fixes that require implementation
- ongoing management opportunities
- explicit "do not change" items where current systems are adequate

## Routing rules

Route findings to:

- website buyer friction → Revenue Lab / Website Sales Review
- software overspend → Software Cost Check
- uncontrolled AI → AI Usage & Risk Review
- dormant leads / quotes → Lost Lead Recovery
- invoice chasing → Payment Chaser
- quote follow-up → Quote Follow-Up Assistant
- supplier chasing → Supplier Quote Assistant
- reporting burden → Management Briefing Assistant
- recurring staff questions → Staff Helpdesk
- document retyping → Document Processing Assistant
- critical person dependency → Business Memory & Continuity
- cross-system manual work → integration / automation sprint
- unique operational requirement → custom system discovery

## Pricing guardrails

Base review: **from R7,500**.

Increase scope when:

- more than 25 staff require interviews
- more than 10 core systems are in scope
- multiple legal entities / locations are included
- raw transaction data needs significant cleanup
- process observation is required
- extensive documentation must be produced

The review price is separate from implementation.

# 2. Software Cost Check

## Objective

Identify recurring software cost that can be removed, reduced or consolidated without harming operations.

## Required data

- software/subscription list
- latest invoices
- user/licence list
- employee roster
- renewal dates/contracts where available
- payment export where the client wants unknown subscriptions discovered

## Checks

For every product capture:

- product
- owner
- business purpose
- billing frequency
- monthly equivalent cost
- licensed seats
- active seats
- unused seats
- duplicate/overlapping tools
- renewal date
- cancellation notice period
- former-employee access
- criticality
- recommended action

## Savings classification

- confirmed — can be removed now
- likely — subject to owner confirmation
- consolidation opportunity — requires change
- no action — justified spend

Never report projected savings as guaranteed savings.

## Managed monthly service

Monthly:

- reconcile active users against paid seats
- check joiners/leavers
- capture new subscriptions
- review renewals within 90 days
- track price changes
- confirm product owners
- report verified savings

# 3. AI Usage & Risk Review

## Objective

Give management visibility over AI tools, access, cost and ownership without banning useful AI.

## Inventory fields

- tool/vendor
- business owner
- users
- purpose
- data entered
- systems connected
- whether customer/personal/confidential data is involved
- model/vendor terms reviewed
- monthly cost
- approved / conditional / prohibited
- human-review requirement
- exit/revocation process

## Risk levels

**Low:** public/non-sensitive data, no system access, human-reviewed.

**Medium:** internal data or workflow integration with bounded permissions.

**High:** personal/customer/confidential data, write access, customer-facing output, financial or operational actions.

**Critical:** uncontrolled autonomous action, credentials/secrets, payment authority, broad production access or unknown ownership.

## Required output

- AI register
- unapproved tool list
- access/data-risk findings
- overlapping AI spend
- approved-use rules
- human-review rules
- priority remediations

## Monthly management

- review new tool requests
- review high-risk vendor changes
- remove leaver access
- maintain approved tool register
- track spend
- quarterly recertification of high-risk AI workflows

# 4. Lost Lead Recovery

## Objective

Find commercial opportunities already acquired but not properly followed through.

## Inputs

- CRM export
- quote history
- enquiry forms
- sales inbox data where authorised
- prior-customer list
- status / last-contact dates

## Segmentation

Classify each record:

- active
- dormant but recoverable
- lost with reason
- stale / insufficient data
- do not contact
- existing customer reactivation

## Recovery priority

Score:

- recency
- quoted value
- prior engagement
- stated need
- reason for delay
- fit
- consent/contactability

Do not contact records marked do-not-contact or where lawful basis/consent is insufficient.

## Monthly metrics

- contacts attempted
- replies
- reopened opportunities
- meetings booked
- quotes reactivated
- recovered revenue where attributable
- opt-outs
- unresolved objections

# 5. Digital Team operating standard

All managed assistants must have:

- one named business owner
- one narrow job description
- approved data sources
- explicit allowed actions
- explicit prohibited actions
- human escalation path
- stop conditions
- audit trail
- monthly health review

No assistant receives payment authority, unrestricted production access or destructive permissions by default.

## Payment Chaser

Triggers:
- invoice approaching due date
- invoice overdue

Stops:
- paid
- dispute opened
- customer requests human contact
- payment arrangement requires approval

Escalates:
- disputes
- repeated non-response
- high-value / sensitive accounts
- promises to pay missed beyond defined threshold

Monthly KPIs:
- overdue value
- recovered value
- average days outstanding
- human escalations

## Quote Follow-Up Assistant

Triggers:
- quote sent
- no decision after configured interval

Captures:
- timing
- budget
- approval
- scope questions
- competitor/lost reason

Escalates:
- buying intent
- pricing negotiation
- scope change
- complaint

KPIs:
- follow-ups completed
- responses
- reopened opportunities
- won/lost outcomes

## Supplier Quote Assistant

Requires:
- approved supplier list
- RFQ template
- human approval before award

Actions:
- prepare/send RFQ
- chase outstanding responses
- extract price, lead time and terms
- compare responses

Never:
- award supplier
- change approved supplier list
- accept binding terms without human approval

## Management Briefing Assistant

Inputs:
- approved reporting sources only

Output:
- facts
- comparisons
- exceptions
- items requiring attention
- source/time stamp where practical

Never fabricate missing data. Missing data must be reported as missing.

## Staff Helpdesk

Ground answers only in approved company documents.

Escalate:
- HR disputes
- disciplinary matters
- medical/sensitive matters
- policy ambiguity
- requests outside approved knowledge

## Document Processing Assistant

Controls:
- field-level confidence
- validation rules
- duplicate detection
- exception queue
- source-document traceability

Never post uncertain financial/customer data automatically unless confidence and business rules meet approved thresholds.

# 6. Business Memory & Continuity

## Customer promise

**How much of your business exists only in somebody's head?**

## Dependency review

For each critical process record:

- process name
- primary owner
- backup owner
- systems used
- access required
- documents/templates required
- key contacts
- exceptions
- frequency
- financial/operational impact if unavailable
- documented yes/no
- tested yes/no

## Key-person risk score

Rate 0–5:

- business criticality
- single-person dependency
- knowledge documentation gap
- access concentration
- recovery difficulty

Priority percentage = total / 25 x 100.

80–100 = critical continuity risk.

## Continuity deliverables

- critical process register
- key-person dependency map
- system/account ownership map
- backup-owner assignments
- minimum viable SOPs
- leaver/handover checklist
- continuity test plan
- knowledge vault structure

## Safe Exit Check

Before a critical employee leaves:

- inventory systems/accounts
- transfer ownership
- transfer business files
- capture undocumented process knowledge
- reassign customer/supplier relationships
- revoke access on agreed date
- recover devices
- remove unnecessary licences
- test backup owner access

## Monthly / quarterly management

- update staff/process ownership
- review new systems
- review access ownership
- refresh critical SOPs
- test selected continuity scenarios
- run leaver checks
- report unresolved key-person risks

# 7. Delivery quality rules

Softkom must:

- separate measured facts from estimates
- state assumptions
- never invent client data
- show evidence for material findings
- avoid promising guaranteed savings/revenue
- protect client credentials and confidential data
- minimise collected data
- keep human approval for high-impact actions
- log changes to recurring managed workflows
- confirm scope before using client systems

A diagnostic is complete only when the client can answer:

1. What is costing us money/time?
2. How large is the problem?
3. What should we fix first?
4. What will Softkom do?
5. What should we leave alone?
6. What needs ongoing management?
