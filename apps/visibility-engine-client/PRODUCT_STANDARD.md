# Visibility Engine Product Standard

## North Star

Visibility Engine must be a commercially superior managed-visibility product, not a clone of any competitor.

The product should answer five questions better than a conventional SEO/content platform:

1. What should we publish next?
2. Why is that topic commercially worth pursuing?
3. Is the client comfortable approving it?
4. Did it publish reliably and perform in search / AI visibility?
5. Did it create qualified leads, opportunities or revenue?

## Core Differentiators

### 1. Revenue-first, not content-volume-first
Do not optimise for article count. Prioritise opportunities by:
- commercial intent
- fit to the client's offer
- SERP weakness
- funnel clarity
- probability of producing qualified business

### 2. Funnel attribution built in
Every page should be traceable through:
Search / AI visibility -> landing page -> CTA -> funnel -> lead -> meeting -> paid engagement -> revenue

### 3. Managed-service workflow
The product is designed for clients who want results without operating an SEO platform themselves.

Client actions should be limited to:
- connect website/data sources
- review strategy
- approve/reject content
- see publishing status
- see visibility and business results

Softkom manages the operational complexity.

### 4. Better approvals
Approval must support:
- approve
- request changes
- inline comments
- revision history
- scheduled publish date
- who approved what and when

### 5. Better publishing reliability
Publishing must include:
- provider connection health
- pre-publish validation
- retries
- clear failure reason
- rollback/version history where supported
- post-publish verification
- broken-link and metadata checks

### 6. Search + AI visibility
Track:
- Google impressions/clicks/CTR/position
- landing page performance
- topic clusters
- branded vs non-branded visibility
- AI assistant mentions/citations where measurable
- competitor coverage

Do not present unverified AI visibility as certainty.

### 7. Better recommendations
The system should explain:
- why a topic is recommended
- what business problem it maps to
- what offer/funnel it should feed
- what competitors currently cover
- what angle the client can own
- what evidence/source material is required

### 8. Client-ready reporting
Monthly reporting should show:
- what was researched
- what was created
- what was published
- what gained visibility
- what generated leads
- what contributed to revenue
- what should happen next

### 9. Multi-tenant safety
Required:
- organisation isolation
- site isolation
- role-based access
- audit logging
- encrypted external credentials
- no cross-client data leakage
- production-safe demo/test data handling

### 10. Premium UX
The product should feel finished:
- fast onboarding
- responsive desktop/mobile
- strong empty states
- clear error handling
- no fake metrics
- polished typography/layout
- clear next actions
- minimal client jargon

## V1 Acceptance Criteria

A real client must be able to:

1. Sign in and see only their organisation.
2. Connect one website.
3. See researched opportunities with reasoning.
4. Open a proposed content item.
5. Approve it or request changes.
6. See publishing status.
7. Open the live published URL.
8. See search metrics when connected.
9. See attributed leads for that content where tracking exists.
10. Receive a monthly summary that connects visibility activity to business outcomes.

## Non-goals for V1

- Mass content generation quotas.
- Automated link buying.
- Fake AI visibility scores.
- Customer-facing billing before the core workflow is proven.
- Broad CMS support before WordPress works extremely well.
- Vanity dashboards without actionable next steps.

## Product Rule

If a feature does not improve strategy, quality, reliability, visibility, lead generation or revenue attribution, it is secondary.
